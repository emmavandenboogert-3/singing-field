Welcome to The Singing Field

I'm so happy you're here.

The Singing Field is a living field of aliveness, in which we connect through sound and songs with what's possible. It's co-created with many amazing people along the way.

I'm surprised by what has been unfolding. The circles have become a place of raw expression, of feeling our feelings, of sacred presence, deep celebration of life, and dancing in the darkness, together.

I want you there, because I think it matters. It matters to keep showing up for the more beautiful world our hearts know is possible. And sing this new world into being.

We sing, for all that is lost.
Keeping it in our hearts.
We sing, for all that will be.
Through the shattering storms,
light and free.

I'm looking forward to singing with you, especially if you usually don't sing. That was me.

Don't hesitate to reach out to me if you have any questions or would like us to come and play in your space.

With reverence for life,
Emma
